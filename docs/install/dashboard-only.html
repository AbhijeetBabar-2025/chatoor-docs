Install the dashboard

This page covers the dashboard-only install. You get the Scanner, Journal, Rewind, Paper trade, Trading desk, and Learn tabs. You do not get written reports or the AI chat window — for those, follow the full install instead.

What you need:

  • A Mac, Windows 10 or 11, or Linux computer
  • The install line from your invitation email

The install line is personal to you. It is not printed here. Find it in the email that invited you to the Chatoor pilot.

On a Mac​

  1. Open Terminal. Press ⌘ Command + Space, type Terminal, and press Return. A window with a blinking cursor opens.

  2. Paste your install line. Copy the line from your invitation email, then press ⌘ Command + V to paste it into Terminal. Press Return.

    The line you paste looks similar to this (yours will be different):

    YOUR-INSTALL-LINE-FROM-EMAIL

  3. Wait for the install to finish. You will see messages like these appear:

    Downloading Chatoor...
    Chatoor is installed.
    Open it any time by double-clicking "Chatoor" on your desktop.

    Nothing asks for a password. If you see a line that starts with "Chatoor install stopped", go to If something goes wrong.

  4. Enter your email and key. The first time the app opens, it asks for the email address your invitation was sent to, and the key from that email (for example: SA-K7QM-2XPA-9RTD-WC4H). Type them in and press Return. A key works on up to three computers.

On Windows​

  1. Open PowerShell. Click Start, type PowerShell, and click Windows PowerShell. A window with a blinking cursor opens.

  2. Paste your install line. Copy the line from your invitation email, then right-click inside the PowerShell window to paste it (or press Ctrl + V). Press Enter.

    The line you paste looks similar to this (yours will be different):

    YOUR-INSTALL-LINE-FROM-EMAIL

  3. Wait for the install to finish. You will see a message when it is done. A second window opens and starts the app.

  4. Enter your email and key from your invitation email, the same as on a Mac.

On Linux​

Open a terminal window. Paste the Mac line from your invitation email and press Return. Follow steps 3 and 4 from the Mac section above.

Opening it again​

Double-click Chatoor on your desktop.

  • Mac: Chatoor opens in its own window. The Chatoor icon appears in the Dock. Close the window when you are done — your data is saved automatically.
  • Windows: Your web browser opens and shows the Chatoor dashboard. A small separate window keeps the app running in the background. Keep that small window open while you use Chatoor. Close it when you are done.

Updating​

Paste the same line from your invitation email into Terminal (Mac) or PowerShell (Windows) again. It installs the newest version and keeps your journal, watchlist, and holdings.

On Windows, if Chatoor is open when you paste the line, the installer closes it first. Open it again from the desktop when the update finishes.

Removing it​

Paste your install line again, but change the word install to uninstall near the end of the line. Paste the changed line into Terminal (Mac) or PowerShell (Windows) and press Return.

The installer stops Chatoor if it is running, removes the app, and removes the desktop icon. It then asks whether to delete your saved data (your journal, watchlist, holdings, and any saved keys). Press Return to keep your data, or type y and press Return to delete it. Kept data comes back if you install again later.

If something goes wrong​

"That email and key don't match"? Check both against your invitation email. The key works with or without dashes and in upper or lower case.

"Already in use on 3 computers"? Contact the person who invited you.

"Key turned off" or "This key ended on [date]"? Contact the person who invited you. The key screen also shows a Remove the app from this computer option.

"Couldn't check your key for 14 days"? Connect to the internet and open the app again.

"Couldn't reach the download page" or "the download didn't finish"? Check your internet connection, then paste the install line again. Work or school networks sometimes block downloads — try from a home network.

"Doesn't match its published checksum"? The download was damaged in transit and nothing was installed. Paste the line again. If this keeps happening, let us know.

"The browser says 'This site can't be reached'"? The small background window was closed. Double-click the desktop icon to restart the app.

"Doesn't know where to download from"? You may have an install line from somewhere other than your invitation email. Use only the line from your invitation.

Windows shows "running scripts is disabled"? You pasted only part of the line. Copy the whole line from the invitation email, starting from the very beginning, and paste it again.

Still stuck? Copy everything shown in the Terminal or PowerShell window and send it to us. It does not contain anything personal.
