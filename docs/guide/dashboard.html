Dashboard

The dashboard is Chatoor's main window. It lets you look up stocks, read analysis, track ideas, and practise.

Opening Chatoor​

Double-click the Chatoor icon on your desktop. The app opens in its own window.

:::tip Opening from Claude You can also ask Claude: Open the stock dashboard and it will open the app for you. :::

The Chatoor mark at the top left — a white trishul on violet — takes you back to Start from anywhere in the app.

Tabs across the top​

TabWhat it does
StartA six-step path for new users. Steps tick off as you go. Once the first five are done, the app opens on Scanner instead.
ScannerLook up stocks and ETFs by symbol and read their analysis.
Paper tradeA pretend-money account. Enter trades at today's prices and see how they perform.
Trading deskToday's key price levels, volume, and a position size calculator. Off by default — turn it on from Start → Day trading tools.
PortfolioTrack what you really own. Off by default — turn it on from Start → Track what you own.
JournalEvery idea you have tracked, with how each one turned out.
RewindPractise on past market data.
LearnPlain-English explanations of every number and label in the app.

Scanner​

The Scanner lets you look up stocks and ETFs by their ticker symbol.

  1. Type a symbol (for example AAPL, GLD, or RELIANCE.NS) and press Enter. It becomes a chip. Add up to 20 symbols.
  2. Press Scan.
  3. Each stock or ETF gets a card showing:
    • its price and 6-month change
    • the score gauge and label (Positive signals, Mixed signals, or Negative signals)
    • the track record: how often the tool's past labels turned out to be right
    • up to three plain-English points from its strongest factors, such as "Very profitable" or "Expensive for its profits" — click one to learn what it means
    • seven factor bars behind Show the 7 factors, showing what pushed the score up or down — tick Show factor details above the cards to see them on every card
    • warnings, such as an upcoming earnings report or a broad market downturn
  4. Click a card to open its full analysis (see the Analysis panel section below).

Your list of symbols is saved automatically. Next time you open the app, the Scanner scans them straight away. Use Sort by to order cards by score, momentum, or symbol.

Results coming up (above the cards) lists the stocks in your watchlist and portfolio that are due to report results in the next two weeks. Click one to open its analysis.

Filter the stocks you follow (above the cards) narrows your scanned stocks down to ones that match conditions you set — for example, a Positive label, a rising trend, and results within 14 days. Add other symbols to screen them too. Find matches lists the ones that fit. Save and alert me keeps the filter running: while the app is open, it checks every 15 minutes and tells you when a stock starts or stops matching.

Track this idea​

Press Track this idea on a card to save it to your Journal. You say what you expect over the next 30 days (whether you think it will beat, match, or lag its index) and how sure you are (50% to 100%). You can tick a short checklist and add a one-line reason. After 30 days the Journal shows how both your view and the tool's turned out.

Guess first​

Tick Guess first (above the cards) to hide the tool's score, label, and summary before you read a card. Cards show only the numbers, with a Make my call button. Hover over any number's name — P/E, ROE, RSI, and so on — to see what it means. Once you have made your call, or pressed Show the tool's view, the card shows the tool's view as usual. The app remembers this setting.

Options view​

The Stocks | Options switch at the top of the search box scans the same symbols for their options instead. Each card shows:

  • the move needed by the next expiry about a week away, such as ±4.2%, and the price range that implies
  • three reads on what option prices say: whether they are pricey, about fair, or cheap next to the stock's recent swings; whether an event such as earnings seems priced in; and whether traders are paying extra for protection against a fall

Cards in Options view have no score and no label. They describe what option prices imply. Click a card to open the full options panel on the right. Click Open the stock analysis inside that panel to switch to the stock's usual analysis. Options data covers US-listed symbols only.

Click any card and a panel slides in from the right. Press Esc, the close button, or click outside it to close it.

Each fact appears once in the panel. After you scan, the app fetches what the panel needs in the background, so opening a card is quick.

Today: your daily digest​

On the first launch each day, Chatoor opens Today: a calm overview before you start. It is always available under the newspaper icon at the top right.

  • The market: the main indexes and how they have moved today
  • Your stocks: each one you hold or watch, with its last saved price, the change since the previous scan, and its data label
  • Worth a look today: ideas ready for a look-back, results coming up, and your paper account
  • News that may matter: headlines from several news sources, ranked by how relevant they are to your stocks and to broad market topics such as results, interest rates, and trade
  • Most traded today: the busiest stocks today, with their data label if you have scanned them
  • One idea for today: a short, sourced quote from a well-known investor or trader, with a question that turns it into practice

Untick Show this each morning to stop it opening on its own.

Your profile​

The person icon at the top right opens your profile.

  • Whose copy this is: your email, the version running, when your key ends, the day you started
  • Your record: how often your own calls were right, next to the tool on the same ideas; your Rewind rounds; your paper trade return; and, if you use the Trading desk, how many of your logged trades had a stop written down
  • The last 30 days and the 30 before: ideas tracked, Rewind rounds, and how often you were right in Rewind

Your profile reads only files on your computer. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Learn​

The Learn tab explains every number and label in the app in plain English, one topic at a time. Topics include the score, stock factors, fund factors, price and trend, risk, and your results. Each card starts with one short sentence, and many include a small diagram. More opens the full explanation. Search looks across every topic. Hover over a factor name or any small i icon anywhere in the app to see a short preview; click to jump to the full card.

Portfolio​

The Portfolio tab tracks what you really own. It is off by default — turn it on from Start → Track what you own. See Portfolio and paper trade for adding holdings and tracking gains and losses.

Good to know​

  • Privacy. The app only accepts connections from your own computer. Other devices and websites cannot reach it.
  • Prices are fetched fresh with each scan. Cards appear one by one as each result comes back. Each card says when its price is from. Prices come from Yahoo Finance and can be up to 15 minutes behind.
  • Narrow window. On a small screen the tabs move to a bar at the bottom, with an icon for each.
  • Light or dark. The app follows your system setting. Use the button in the top-right corner to switch.
  • Empty pages explain themselves. Before you have scanned, tracked, or logged anything, each page shows a short explanation and a button for what to do next.
  • If something goes wrong, you see a plain message where it happened, with a Try again button and a Report a problem link. See Troubleshooting.
